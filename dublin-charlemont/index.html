Dublin after work, from the canal

Authentic locals' pubs, casual dinner and genuine Irish gifts, all walkable from the hotel. Temple Bar is skipped on purpose.

Researched 14–15 Nov 2026 · walk times from the Hilton · no bookings made

Know before you go

Base: Hilton Dublin, Charlemont Place, Dublin 2, D02 A893 — south side of the Grand Canal, Portobello/Ranelagh edge. The Charlemont Luas (Green line) is beside the hotel; two stops to St Stephen's Green for the gift shops.
Rhythm: Back at the hotel about 4:30–5pm Mon–Thu after the 30-min Uber from training. Sunset is mid-afternoon in mid-November, so evenings start in the dark. Taxis: FreeNow is the usual Dublin hail app; Uber also books licensed taxis.
Walk times: Measured from the hotel coordinates (53.33118, −6.25918). OpenStreetMap walking-route distance (OSRM) ÷ 4.8 km/h (80 m/min), rounded. For a few Baggot / Merrion / Grafton destinations the mapped route looked like a canal-crossing detour, so those are shown as a range (straight-line × 1.35 as the low end, OSRM as the high end). Tap Directions for a live Google walking estimate.
Verification: ✔ own site hours or music read on the official site on 14 Nov 2026. ◐ Google / listing rating or hours from a public Google listing (via Exa or a similar mirror) the same day. ✖ unverified confirm before going. Nothing here is a booking.
Avoid the Temple Bar tourist-trap pubs. The famous Merrion Row O'Donoghue's is not the same as O'Donoghue's on Suffolk Street — go to 15 Merrion Row if you go at all.

Suggested evenings

One evening is for gifts. The rest stay close to the canal. Swap freely.

Sun15

Arrival, canal, early trad

Land ~10:54 Irish time. Check in, walk the Grand Canal (it's at the door), nap if you need it. Easiest pint: The Barge is 2 minutes away (convenient sports/live-music pub, not a quiet local). Better authentic option: Devitt's has official Sunday trad from 1pm, plus food all day. If you still have legs, O'Donoghue's (Merrion Row, ~15–20 min, more touristy) lists Sunday sessions all day. Optional afternoon gift loop — Avoca is open until 7pm Sunday. Early night.

Mon16

Neighbourhood pint

Camden Kitchen and Richmond are closed Monday. Dinner at The Hill (8 min, food + cocktails, opens 2pm) or GiGi (casual Italian, 12 min, dinner from 5pm) or pub grub at Devitt's. Then the 6-min walk to J. O'Connell's (opens 4pm) for the week's best local pint.

Tue17

Camden dinner + trad (or an Old Fashioned)

Camden Kitchen from 5pm (10 min; last booking 9:30pm) — the best nearby Irish neighbourhood dinner. Then Devitt's trad from 6:30pm, or knock in at The Sitting Room at Delahunt (9 min, classic cocktails from 5pm, closed Sun–Mon) if you want an Old Fashioned.

Wed18

Portobello dinner, then Baggot / Leeson

Richmond early-evening menu until 6:30pm (2/3 courses €44/€55; last orders 9pm) — 6 min, same street as O'Connell's. Then Hartigan's (~10 min; possible midweek session, unverified) or walk on to Doheny & Nesbitt (official trad from 6:45pm, food all day, moderate tourist level) or Toners next door.

Thu19

Gift evening ⭐

Leave the hotel around 5pm. Luas from Charlemont, 2 stops to St Stephen's Green (or a 15–25 min walk). Hit Irish Design Shop and Article first (both close 6pm), then Celtic Whiskey Shop and Kilkenny (both until 7pm Thursday), finish at Avoca (until 7). If you only need whiskey, Redmond's of Ranelagh is a 10-min walk from the hotel and stays open until 9pm Tue–Thu. Pint after at Kehoe's (snugs, no music, right by the shops) or O'Donoghue's trad from 8pm. Sheridans is already closed on weeknights (5pm) — do it Sunday if you want cheese.

Fri 20: flight home around 1pm. Only try a morning shop if gifts didn't happen — Avoca opens 9am, Kilkenny 10am, Celtic Whiskey 10:30am — and leave plenty of time for the airport.

Gift plan

Genuine Irish design, knitwear, whiskey and food — not souvenir-shop tat. Dublin's old "late Thursday" habit is now modest: only some shops stay open to 7pm. Irish Design Shop and Article still close at 6pm every weekday, and Sheridans closes at 5pm Mon–Fri, so go straight after work.

Recommended: Thursday after work. Leave the hotel around 5pm. Take the Luas Green line from Charlemont (beside the hotel), 2 stops to St Stephen's Green, then walk — or walk the whole way in about 15–25 minutes. Do the 6pm closers first.

  1. Irish Design Shop (41 Drury Street) — independent makers, designed and made in Ireland. Own site: Mon–Sat 10am–6pm, Sun 12–5pm. ~20–24 min walk / short Luas+walk.
  2. Article (Powerscourt Townhouse, South William Street) — Irish design and homeware, almost next door. Own site: Mon–Sat 10:30am–6pm, Sun 12–5pm.
  3. Celtic Whiskey Shop (27–28 Dawson Street) — serious Irish whiskey, knowledgeable staff, the best selection in town. Google listing (15 Nov 2026): Mon–Sat 10:30am–7pm, Sun 12:30–6pm. ★4.7, 1,300 Google reviews. Official homepage lists the same hours. See also the whiskey shops section; Redmond's is closer to the hotel if you only need a couple of bottles.
  4. Kilkenny Design (6 Nassau Street) — flagship Irish craft, jewellery, design, café upstairs. Own site: Mon–Wed & Fri 10am–6:30pm, Thu 10am–7pm, Sat–Sun 10am–6pm.
  5. Avoca (11–13 Suffolk Street) — knits, throws, homeware and an excellent food hall (the most useful food-gift stop). Own site: Mon–Sat 9am–7pm, Sun 10am–7pm.

Pint afterwards: Kehoe's is around the corner from Sheridans / Grafton, or O'Donoghue's trad from 8pm.

Sunday arrival

Jet-lag friendly, all near the hotel, with Sunday hours checked where possible.

1. Canal walk. The Grand Canal is at the hotel door. A flat, easy reset after the American Airlines flight — west toward Portobello Harbour (Lower Deck, O'Connell's) or east toward Leeson Bridge (Hartigan's is closed Sunday).
2. Trad without a late night. Devitt's official Sunday session starts at 1pm, and the kitchen is open. O'Donoghue's Merrion Row lists Sunday music all day (more touristy, ~15–20 min). Doheny & Nesbitt has official Sunday trad from 6:45pm if you get a second wind. Hartigan's is closed.
3. Dinner. Devitt's (all day), The Hill (from noon Sunday), GiGi (OpenTable: Sunday dinner 4:30–9pm), Richmond (Sunday lunch 12:30–2:30 and dinner 5–8pm last orders), or The Barge 2 minutes away. Camden Kitchen is closed Sunday.
4. Optional gift afternoon. If you're not wrecked: Sheridans 12–5, Irish Design Shop 12–5, Article 12–5, Kilkenny 10–6, Celtic Whiskey 12:30–6 (Google hours), Avoca 10–7. That would free Thursday for another pub night.
