Names for a Future Brophy

Ten boy names and ten girl names drawn from the sea, the stars, the Klamath Basin, the Carolinas, and the people who electrified the world. Each one is checked for meaning, flow with Brophy, and real 2025 popularity.

Made for Frank Brophy · October 17, 2026 · Popularity is from U.S. Social Security Administration baby-name data for 2025, the latest year SSA has published. Meanings were checked against Behind the Name and other references listed in Sources.

Congratulations, first of all
Frank and Brielle married on October 4 and honeymooned in Oceanside. What a start. This list isn’t a countdown; it’s a fun shortlist to keep in your pocket for whenever you’re ready. Every name ties back to something real: seven years at sea as an Electronics Officer out of Kings Point, a Klamath Falls childhood, the Arizona desert, a possible Carolina move, and a career keeping the power flowing to AI.
How to use it
Say each one out loud with “Brophy” after it, then try it the way you’d actually yell it across a beach or a dog park. Every list has a mix of Familiar names, Distinctive ones, and one Bold pick.

2. Boy names

Ordered from most to least common. You’ll find navigators, Carolina connections, and a couple of names straight from the physics of electricity.

Hudson Brophy Familiar

HUD-sun BRO-fee · English surname

Meaning
From an English surname meaning “son of Hudde.” Its best-known bearer is the English explorer and navigator Henry Hudson (1570–1611). (Behind the Name)
Why it fits
A name carried by one of history’s great navigators, for a Kings Point grad who spent seven years at sea. It’s sturdy and classic, and it’s easy to picture on a kid who likes to explore.
With Brophy
HUD-sun BRO-fee. The soft “n” hands off cleanly to the B, and the stress pattern (DUM-da DUM-da) feels steady.
Popularity
#17 in 2025 (8,583 babies)
Kai Brophy Familiar

KYE BRO-fee · Hawaiian (also Frisian/Scandinavian)

Meaning
In Hawaiian, kai means “sea.” The European Kai (as in Andersen’s The Snow Queen) is a separate name of uncertain meaning. (Behind the Name (Hawaiian) · Behind the Name (European))
Why it fits
One syllable of pure ocean for a guy who loves the beach and honeymooned in Oceanside. It’s short, warm, and easy to call across a beach.
With Brophy
KYE BRO-fee. Short then long, crisp, with no sounds running together.
Popularity
#93 in 2025 (3,768 babies)
Finn Brophy Familiar

FIN BRO-fee · Irish / Old Norse

Meaning
The Old Irish form of Fionn, from finn “white, blessed” (the legendary hero Fionn mac Cumhaill, or Finn McCool). A separate Old Norse Finnr meant “Sámi, person from Finland.” (Behind the Name: Finn · Fionn · Finnr)
Why it fits
An Irish first name to go with the Irish surname Brophy, and it’s hard not to picture a dorsal fin cutting through the swell off the coast. It suits someone who is happiest near the water.
With Brophy
FIN BRO-fee. Short and punchy. The “n” stays separate from the B, so nothing blurs.
Popularity
#206 in 2025 (1,702 babies)
Rhett Brophy Familiar

RET BRO-fee · Dutch, via English

Meaning
An Anglicized form of the Dutch surname de Raedt, from raet “advice, counsel.” Margaret Mitchell made it famous with Rhett Butler in Gone with the Wind (1936). (Behind the Name)
Why it fits
Rhett Butler is probably Charleston’s most famous fictional son, so this one is a nod to a possible Lowcountry chapter. A name meaning “counsel” also suits a practical, plan-ahead dad.
With Brophy
RET BRO-fee. The hard “t” makes a clean stop before the B: a crisp one-plus-two rhythm.
Popularity
#188 in 2025 (1,936 babies)
Maxwell Brophy Familiar

MAKS-wel BRO-fee · Scottish surname

Meaning
From the Scottish surname meaning “Mack’s stream” (Mack, from Magnus, plus Old English wille “well, stream”). Physicist James Clerk Maxwell (1831–1879) worked out the theory of electromagnetism. (Behind the Name)
Why it fits
Maxwell’s equations sit underneath everything Frank has kept running, from shipboard radios and radar to data center power. “Max” is a friendly everyday nickname.
With Brophy
MAKS-wel BRO-fee. The liquid “l” glides straight into Brophy, and the whole name has a balanced 2+2 beat.
Popularity
#189 in 2025 (1,924 babies)
Orion Brophy Distinctive

oh-RYE-un BRO-fee · Greek (possibly Akkadian)

Meaning
Meaning uncertain: possibly from Greek horion “boundary, limit,” or from Akkadian Uru-anna “light of the heavens.” It’s the name of the hunter constellation. (Behind the Name)
Why it fits
Orion is the winter sky’s big landmark. Two of its stars, Rigel and Bellatrix, are on the official list of 57 navigational stars, which makes this a nice salute to long night watches at sea.
With Brophy
oh-RYE-un BRO-fee. Three syllables into two flows well. The O sounds echo a little but don’t rhyme.
Popularity
#334 in 2025 (1,012 babies)

Edison Brophy Distinctive

ED-ih-sun BRO-fee · English surname

Meaning
From an English surname meaning either “son of Eda” or “son of Adam.” Inventor Thomas Edison (1847–1931) is its most famous bearer. (Behind the Name · Pearl Street Station (Wikipedia))
Why it fits
Edison’s Pearl Street Station (1882) was one of the first central power plants, the distant ancestor of the grids and switchgear a data center Chief Engineer looks after. It’s inventive and hands-on, like its namesake.
With Brophy
ED-ih-sun BRO-fee. Rolls like Hudson: the soft “n” and then a clean B.
Popularity
#870 in 2025 (276 babies)
Raleigh Brophy Distinctive

RAH-lee BRO-fee · English place name

Meaning
From an English surname taken from a place name meaning “red clearing” or “roe deer clearing” in Old English. The North Carolina capital is named for Sir Walter Raleigh (1552–1618), courtier, poet and explorer. (Behind the Name)
Why it fits
Two themes in one: a seafaring explorer, and a city on the Carolinas shortlist. If you end up there, it doubles as a hometown story.
With Brophy
RAH-lee BRO-fee. Both names end in an “ee” sound, which gives it a bouncy, sing-song feel. Say it out loud a few times to see if you like it.
Popularity
Outside the SSA top 1000 in 2025 (64 babies in the SSA file)
Faraday Brophy Bold pick

FAIR-uh-day BRO-fee · Irish surname

Meaning
An Anglicized form of the Irish surname Ó Fearadaigh, “descendant of Fearadach,” an old Irish personal name meaning “manly” (from fear, “man”). It’s best known from physicist Michael Faraday (1791–1867). (Wiktionary · Woulfe, Irish Names and Surnames (1923) · Fearadhach)
Why it fits
Faraday discovered electromagnetic induction, the principle behind the generators and transformers that deliver every watt a data center uses. It’s a bold, electrical choice, and its Irish roots match Brophy.
With Brophy
FAIR-uh-day BRO-fee. A rolling 3+2 rhythm, and the open “day” vowel hands off cleanly to the B.
Popularity
Not in the 2025 SSA data (the SSA file leaves out names given to fewer than 5 babies)

4. The Brophy sound check

Brophy is said BRO-fee: two syllables, stress up front, starting on a B-R blend and ending on an “ee.” (It’s an Anglicized form of the Irish Ó Bróithe, which is why Irish first names like Finn and Faraday feel at home.) Here are the filters used for every name:

  • No first names ending in a strong B or P sound (Jacob, Caleb, Philip). The two lip sounds run together into “Caleb-Brophy.” Juniper is the only borderline case, and it’s noted on its card.
  • No first names starting with Br- (Brooks, Bryce, Bristol). The surname already brings the Br.
  • Watch out for the “-o” rhyme. Names like Marlowe, Ronan or Milo echo “Bro” (“Marlowe Brophy”), so they were left off even though Marlowe, “remnants of a lake,” was tempting for Klamath Lake.
  • “-ee” endings double up. Names ending in an “ee” sound (Riley, Bodhi) bounce off Brophy’s own “-fee.” Two on this list, Raleigh and Lucy, do this, and it’s flagged so you can judge.
  • Rhythm. With a two-syllable, front-stressed surname, one-, two- and three-syllable first names all work. Endings on a consonant (Rhett, Hudson) or an open “-a” (Stella, Vega) hand off most cleanly.

5. How popularity was sourced
  • Source: the U.S. Social Security Administration’s national baby-name file (names.zip, file yob2025.txt), which covers 2025 births, the latest year SSA has released. SSA’s own baby names page currently shows the 2025 top 10 (Liam and Olivia at #1).
  • How it was pulled: ssa.gov blocks automated downloads, so the exact SSA file was taken from the Internet Archive’s June 2, 2026 capture of ssa.gov. Its top 10 matches the live SSA page exactly, and the ranks match Behind the Name’s US 2025 list, give or take a slot where names are tied.
  • Ranks are positions in SSA’s list, which sorts by number of babies and breaks exact ties alphabetically. A few names here tie exactly with a neighbor: Lucy (6,176) ties Ellie, Marina (488) ties Emelia, Ocean (345) ties two others, and Edison (276) ties three. Some sites therefore show them one to three places higher.
  • Outside the top 1000: SSA’s published rankings stop at 1000, so Raleigh (boys) and Vega (girls) are listed as “outside the SSA top 1000,” with their actual 2025 counts from the SSA file. Faraday doesn’t appear at all, because SSA leaves out any name given to fewer than 5 babies of a sex in a year.
  • Counts are by sex: Morgan, for example, ranks #559 for boys but is used more for girls, and Raleigh also had 104 girls in 2025.
