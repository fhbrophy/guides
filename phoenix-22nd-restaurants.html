🍽️ Good restaurants near 4800 N 22nd St

Biltmore / Camelback Corridor, Phoenix · researched Mon Nov 9, 2026

Top picks

1. Glai Baan (1.6 mi): the strongest all-around pick and fully casual. It's Michelin-recommended and on both the Eater 38 and Phoenix New Times Top 50, with Thai food that has real punch (khao soi, son-in-law eggs). Book dinner by phone.

2. Tratto (a short walk, at 20th St): the best Italian in metro Phoenix per New Times, in a relaxed rustic room. Open Tue–Sat only. For something faster and more casual, Pizzeria Bianco is in the same building and open daily.
